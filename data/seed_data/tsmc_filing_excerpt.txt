RISK FACTORS — GEOPOLITICAL AND CROSS-STRAIT EXPOSURE (Simulated TSMC 20-F Excerpt)

Our operations depend heavily on fabs located in Taiwan, including advanced-node facilities in Hsinchu and Tainan Science Parks. Escalation of cross-strait tensions, military activity in the Taiwan Strait, or disruptions to regional logistics could materially affect our ability to manufacture, package, and ship semiconductors.

We monitor PLA naval activity, export control developments, and customer requests for geographic diversification of supply. While we are expanding capacity in Arizona, Japan, and Germany, a significant majority of leading-edge wafer output remains in Taiwan for the foreseeable planning horizon.

Forward-looking statements in this excerpt note that diversification timelines may accelerate if geopolitical risk premiums persist. Customers in the EU and United States remain dependent on TSMC for sub-7nm production for AI accelerators, mobile SoCs, and automotive ADAS chips.

We cannot assure investors that contingency shipping routes, inventory buffers, or alternative packaging partners would fully offset a prolonged Taiwan Strait disruption. Any sustained blockade or kinetic incident near our fabs could cause severe revenue impact and global semiconductor shortages.
